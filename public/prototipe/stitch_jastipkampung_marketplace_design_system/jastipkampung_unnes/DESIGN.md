## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, community-driven, and structured retail environment for campus peer-to-peer culinary pre-orders. Tailored for university students seeking authentic hometown dishes, it merges the reliability of modern e-commerce with the tactile warmth of Indonesian regional culinary culture.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Artisanal & Grounded:** Rooted in regional culinary roots, evoking nostalgia through rich earthen tones, spice hues, and clear, editorial typography.
- **Campus Trustworthy:** High structural clarity, transparent peer verification badges, and structured milestone tracking to assure student safety and on-time pickup.
- **Efficient & Crisp:** Compact information density akin to Asian marketplace standards (ratings, quota counters, batch cutoffs), presented with refined whitespace and balanced alignment.

### Design Style
The system adopts an **editorial marketplace** aesthetic. It avoids heavy skeletal neumorphism or distracting retro effects in favor of crisp white surface cards, warm tinted backdrops, hairline borders, and calculated micro-shadows. Content is king: food imagery, origin provenance badges, and batch fulfillment counters are given immediate structural priority.

## Colors

The color palette centers on warm spice, terracotta earth, and sunlit cream tones to create a culinary atmosphere while preserving high contrast and accessibility.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas / Page Background (`#FDF8F5`):** Warm cream canvas that softens screen glare and differentiates the viewport from cold utilitarian platforms.
- **Top Bar & Navigation Surface (`#FEFAF7`):** Elevated header tint with hairline border separation.
- **Container Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure crisp white for cards, sheets, and modular panels to ensure maximum legibility for food photography and text.
- **Secondary Surfaces (`#F5EBE4`):** Warm sand tone for progress bar tracks, disabled fills, and secondary icon wrappers.
- **Accent Soft Surfaces (`#FFF5F1`, `#FCEAE4`):** Subtle blush and peach highlights for hover states, selected radio/checkbox tiles, and tag backgrounds.

### Primary, Secondary & Brand Tones
- **Muted Spice Brand Anchor (`#8C5D42`):** Primary token driving overarching editorial accents, key metadata icons, and secondary buttons.
- **Terracotta Accent (`#B5502E`):** Vibrant actionable tone for primary CTAs, active progress fills, and price highlights.
  - Hover: `#9E4527`
  - Active / Focus: `#87381D`

### Functional Accents
- **Rating Star Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Dedicated star fill rating metric with `#FEF3C7` background container and `#92400E` badge text.
- **Verification Emerald (`#065F46` on `#D1FAE5`):** Student verification, trust checkmarks, and successful payment confirmations.
- **Campus Spot Blue (`#1E3A8A` on `#DBEAFE`):** Campus meeting point identifiers (e.g., Gang Pinggir Sekaran, Bundaran BNI UNNES).
- **Urgency / Stock Alert (`#B91C1C` on `#FEE2E2`):** Low quota warnings, closing batch deadlines, and cancellation indicators.

### Line & Dividers
- Hairline Border: `#EADBD1`
- Dashed Structural Divider: `#DCC4B5`

## Typography

The pairing between **Epilogue** and **Manrope** provides a balance between artisanal editorial distinction and crisp retail utility.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Display & Headlines (Epilogue):** Epilogue’s architectural geometry grounds store titles, campaign hero sections, and category headings. Keep tracking tight (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on sizes above 24px.
- **Body & Controls (Manrope):** Manrope handles high-density marketplace tables, metadata listings, and consumer reviews.
- **Numerical & Pricing Formatting:** All currency strings (`Rp 35.000`) and counter badges must render using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain vertical alignment within review columns and shopping carts.
- **Micro-Eyebrows:** Hometown badges (e.g., `ASLI KUDUS`, `PEKALONGAN`) and status tags must use `micro-eyebrow` styling in full uppercase with wider letter spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px base rhythm, adapting fluidly across responsive breakpoints to sustain marketplace efficiency.

### Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width canvas capped at 1280px. 12-column grid with `gutter-desktop` (24px) and minimum `margin-desktop` (48px) safe gutters. Product grids span 4 or 3 columns (e.g., 4 cards per row).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Card grid scales to 2 or 3 items per row.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px horizontal margins. Product cards transition into a 2-column compact grid with reduced internal padding.

### Layout Principles
- Use vertical rhythm multiples of `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px) for item metadata.
- Group contextual modules (e.g., hometown origin badge next to verified badge) with tight 4px or 8px gaps (`space-xs` or `space-sm`).
- Maintain consistent card interior padding: `space-md` (16px) on desktop, scaling to 12px on small mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered warm surfaces, crisp hairline borders, and subtle, low-opacity warm-tinted shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Page canvas (`#FDF8F5`) and inset sections (`#F5EBE4`). Completely shadowless with flat boundary divisions.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Standard product cards, filter bars, and review panels.
  - Border: `1px solid #EADBD1`
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(140, 93, 66, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(140, 93, 66, 0.03)`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Sticky Menus):** Card hover states, sticky header bars (`#FEFAF7`), and floating counter ribbons.
  - Border: `1px solid #DCC4B5`
  - Shadow: `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(140, 93, 66, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(140, 93, 66, 0.04)`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Drawers):** Campus location picker popovers, review photo lightboxes, and cart side-sheets.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.04)`

## Shapes

The design uses a soft roundedness (`0.25rem` / `4px` base scaling) that pairs with the geometric personality of Epilogue and maintains crisp retail alignment.

### Rounding Matrix
- **Controls & Micro-elements (`0.25rem` / `rounded`):** Input fields, form selectors, progress bar indicators, and inline tag pills.
- **Containers & Secondary Cards (`0.5rem` / `rounded-lg`):** Product cards, review summary modules, dropdown menus, and store bio banners.
- **Modals & Hero Modules (`0.75rem` / `rounded-xl`):** Checkout summary card, batch milestone stepper container, and dialogue panels.
- **Pill Elements (`9999px` / `rounded-full`):** Origin badges, star rating tags, user avatars, and step counters.

## Components

### 1. Interactive Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#B5502E`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, font weight 600. On hover: `#9E4527`. On active: `#87381D`. Includes subtle drop shadow `0 2px 4px rgba(181, 80, 46, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary (Outlined):** Border `1.5px solid #8C5D42`, text `#8C5D42`, background transparent. On hover: background `#FFF5F1`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Border `1px solid #EADBD1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1F2937`. On hover: background `#F5EBE4`.
- **Compact Retail Action:** Height 36px, horizontal padding 12px, font size `label-md`.

### 2. Food / Pre-order Marketplace Cards
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface, border `1px solid #EADBD1`, radius `0.5rem`.
- **Header Badges:** Floating top tags for Hometown Origin (`#FDF8F5` background, `#8C5D42` text with `#EADBD1` border) and Batch Schedule (`#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text: "Kirim: Jumat").
- **Visual Display:** Aspect ratio 4:3 food photography with subtle inner border overlay.
- **Quota Progress Element:**
  - Bar track: Height 6px, background `#F5EBE4`, radius `4px`.
  - Bar fill: Terracotta `#B5502E`.
  - Micro-label: `label-md` bold, showing remaining slots (e.g., "Sisa 4 porsi").
- **Vendor Signature:** Inline student store badge displaying `★ 4.9 Toko Mas Budi (Pekalongan)`.

### 3. Shopee-Style Rating & Review Summary Module
- **Rating Score Hero:** High-contrast block with bold numeric score `4.9`, star icon array in `#F59E0B`, and total review counter (`248 ulasan`).
- **Rating Distribution Bars:** 5 rows (5 stars down to 1 star).
  - Track: Background `#F5EBE4`, height 8px, border radius `4px`.
  - Fill: Amber `#F59E0B`.
  - Suffix: Tabular review count and percentage aligned to the right.
- **Review Filter Chips:**
  - Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EADBD1`, text `#4B5563`.
  - Active: Background `#FFF5F1`, border `1.5px solid #B5502E`, text `#B5502E`, font weight 600.
  - Labels: "Semua (248)", "5 Bintang (210)", "Dengan Foto (84)", "Rasa Otentik (62)".

### 4. Order Milestone Stepper (5 Stages)
- **Sequence:**
  1. *Pre-Order Dibuka*
  2. *Batas Pembayaran*
  3. *Belanja di Kampung*
  4. *Perjalanan ke Semarang*
  5. *Siap Diambil di Sekaran*
- **Step Node Indicator:**
  - Completed / Active: 28px circle with `#B5502E` background, white check or step number.
  - Pending: 28px circle with `#F5EBE4` background, `#8C5D42` border, `#6B7280` text.
- **Connecting Lines:** Solid 2px `#B5502E` for cleared milestones; dashed 2px `#DCC4B5` for future phases.

### 5. Form Elements & Input Fields
- **Search Bar (Sekaran Hub):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EADBD1`, placeholder text `#9CA3AF`. Prefix includes campus delivery pin ("Sekaran, UNNES").
- **Focus States:** Border color shifts to `#8C5D42` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(140, 93, 66, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Accent color mapped directly to `#B5502E`. Selected state displays crisp white icons over terracotta fill.
